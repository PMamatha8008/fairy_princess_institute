Skill Development

Beauty & Wellness

Our "Beauty Therapist" course is one of the most sought-after programs among female candidates aged 16–45 years. Offered free of cost through Government and sponsor support, this course empowers women to build successful careers in the beauty industry. Graduates of the course often go on to work in salons, open their own beauty parlors, or provide professional home-based services to clients. This sector offers a perfect opportunity for self-employment and financial independence.

Apparel

The "Sewing Machine Operator" course is a highly popular choice among women looking to enter the Apparel sector. This Government-sponsored free training program enables women to acquire professional tailoring and stitching skills. After successful completion, many candidates launch their own boutiques, take up jobs in garment factories, or offer custom tailoring services from home. The course helps women gain practical skills to become entrepreneurs or skilled employees in the apparel industry.
